Welcome to Hotel Dayal Paradise

Comfort | Serenity | Luxury | Greenery

Dayal Paradise – A plush address of Comfort, Serenity, Luxury & Greenery, is one of the premier hotels in Gomti Nagar Lucknow. Located in the tranquil backdrops of Vipul Khand, Gomti Nagar, amidst Multiplexes, Recreational Parks, and Corporate & Business Houses, it is well connected to the Railway Station, City Bus Terminal & Airport. An ideal abode for Vacations, Conferences, Weddings, or any other events, it is also a preferred family room hotel in Lucknow, complete with excellent facilities for a rewarding stay

BANQUET HALLS
